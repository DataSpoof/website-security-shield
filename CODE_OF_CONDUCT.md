# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.

- Be respectful and constructive.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith; disagree technically, not personally.
- **Security-specific:** do not use this project, its issues, or its discussions to solicit, plan, or share unauthorized attacks against systems you don't own. Keep everything defensive or within authorized-testing scope (see [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md)).

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer in `CITATION.cff`. Maintainers may remove content or contributors that violate this code.
